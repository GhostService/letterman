Letterman Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

Letterman helps high-school students log volunteer hours, get them signed by a supervisor, and find volunteer opportunities posted by local organizations. This page explains what the app collects, who can see it, and how it's protected.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and passwordTo sign in, verify your account, and reset your password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.
Full nameShown on your hour logs and to organizations whose opportunities you sign up for.
Phone, school, grade (optional)Shared only with organizations whose opportunities you sign up for, so they can reach you.
Hour logs: place, address, date, hours, commentsYour volunteer record.
Supervisor's name and drawn signatureProof that the hours were completed.
Your own calendar entriesYour volunteering plans.
Organization name (organization accounts)Shown on the opportunities they post.

Letterman has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and does not access your location. We never sell or rent personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

  • You can see everything in your account.
  • Organizations see your name, email, and the phone, school and grade on your profile only if you sign up for one of their opportunities. They never see your hour logs or signatures.
  • Other students can't see any of your information.
  • The app's administrator can access the data only to run the service: for example, approving organization accounts or helping you when you ask.

Organization accounts are reviewed and approved by the administrator before they can post opportunities or see any student's contact details.

Calendar

If you turn on "Sync to iPhone Calendar", the app adds a calendar called "Volunteering" on your phone with your sign-ups and your own entries. It doesn't read or change your other calendars. Turning sync off or signing out removes that calendar.

How your information is protected

  • All connections use HTTPS encryption.
  • Names, phone numbers, schools, grades, hour logs, signatures and calendar entries are encrypted in our database with AES-256.
  • Once a supervisor signs an hour log, it's locked and can't be changed.
  • On your phone, your login is stored in the iOS Keychain.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, hour logs, signatures, sign-ups and calendar entries. Server backups are kept for up to 7 days before they're overwritten. You can export your hours to a spreadsheet before deleting.

Beta testing

While the app is being tested through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and usage information and share them with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice.

Age

Letterman is meant for high-school students and is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.
